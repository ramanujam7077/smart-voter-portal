Step 1 Eligibility Verification

Understand Your Eligibility Conditions

Ensure you are a citizen of India, will be at least 18 years old on or before the upcoming statutory qualifying date (Jan 1, Apr 1, Jul 1, Oct 1), and are an ordinary resident in the constituency where you wish to vote.

Run our eligibility check to see your qualifying date →
Step 2 Form Selection

Identify the Correct Official Form

Under the Election Commission of India rules, choose the exact form for your circumstance:

  • Form 6: Application for first-time voters or citizens shifting to a new assembly constituency.
  • Form 6A: Application for Non-Resident Indians (Overseas Electors holding valid Indian passports).
  • Form 8: Application for correction of existing details, shifting of residence within constituency, or replacement of EPIC card.
Step 3 Document Readiness

Prepare Required Information and Documents

Scan or photograph clear copies of your documents (PDF/JPG, under 2MB). See the interactive document checklist below to verify what is required vs accepted alternatives.

Step 4 Online Submission

Submit Application through the Official Service

Go to voters.eci.gov.in or download the official Voter Helpline App. Sign in using your mobile number and OTP, complete Form 6, upload your documents, and click Submit.

Save and print the Reference Number generated upon submission. You will need it to track your application.

Step 5 Status Tracking

Follow the Official Status Tracking Process

Your application will be verified by the local Booth Level Officer (BLO) through field verification. You can track progress in real-time on voters.eci.gov.in/track-application using your Reference ID and State.

Step 6 Roll Verification

Verify that Your Name Appears in the Electoral Roll

Once approved, your EPIC number will be generated and your name will be added to the electoral roll. Check your entry online at electoralsearch.eci.gov.in to confirm your designated polling station.
