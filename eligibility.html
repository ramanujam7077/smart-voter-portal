Understanding the 4 Statutory Qualifying Dates

Under Indian Election Law (RPA 1950, Section 14b amended in 2022), you no longer have to wait an entire year to become eligible. The Election Commission of India recognizes January 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1 as qualifying dates.

Citizens who turn 18 in any of these four quarters are eligible to enroll, with advance applications accepted in the quarter before your 18th birthday.
