Ideal if you don't have your Voter ID card with you

{{ t('check_method1_desc') }}

  1. Visit electoralsearch.eci.gov.in and click the "Search by Details" tab.
  2. Enter your Name, Relative's Name, Age or DOB, and Gender.
  3. Select your State, District, and Assembly Constituency.
  4. Enter the Captcha code and click Search to view your designated polling booth.

{{ t('check_method2_title') }}

Fastest & most accurate search method

{{ t('check_method2_desc') }}

  1. Select the "Search by EPIC" tab.
  2. Enter your 10-character EPIC number (e.g., ABC1234567) and select your State.
  3. Enter the Captcha and click Search to retrieve your Part Number, Serial Number, and Polling Station location.

{{ t('check_method3_title') }}

Convenient for smartphone users

{{ t('check_method3_desc') }}

  1. Select the "Search by Mobile" tab.
  2. Enter your registered 10-digit mobile number and State.
  3. Click Send OTP and enter the 6-digit code received via SMS.
  4. View matching voter details linked to your mobile.

What To Do If Something Is Wrong

My Name Is Missing

If your name does not appear, you are NOT eligible to cast a ballot on polling day.

Action: Submit Form 6 on voters.eci.gov.in immediately during continuous updation.

My Details Are Incorrect

If your name is misspelled, photo is outdated, or DOB/address needs correction.

Action: Submit Form 8 on the ECI portal and select "Correction of Entries in Existing Electoral Roll".

I Moved to a New Address

If you shifted your residence within or outside your assembly constituency.

Action: Submit Form 8 (Shifting of Residence) with proof of your new address.

Need personalized assistance from your local electoral office? Call the ECI Toll-Free National Contact Centre at 1950.
